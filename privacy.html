Privacy policy

Goku Studio · last updated 26 November 2026

What Goku Studio is

Goku Studio is a small personal app that makes short AI-generated videos of Goku, a golden retriever, and uploads them to Goku's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is used only by Goku's owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What data it uses

When the owner signs in to connect an account, the platform gives the app an access token. Goku Studio uses those tokens only to:

  • upload Goku's videos, with their titles, captions and hashtags, to Goku's own accounts; and
  • read back the basic details needed to confirm a post worked (for example the video's ID and link, and the privacy settings the platform allows).

On YouTube, the app asks only for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, messages or contacts, and it does not access any other person's data.

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository, or encrypted in the app's own private database. They are used only by the app's scheduled jobs. Videos and their captions are kept privately by the owner.

Sharing

Goku Studio does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Videos are sent only to the platform they are posted on. No advertising or analytics tracking is used, and this website sets no cookies.

Google user data

Goku Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The YouTube upload is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the account's own settings, for example Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once removed, the app can no longer post, and its stored tokens stop working.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/DevKhaira/goku-site/issues.
