Goku Studio

Wisdom, delivered by a rather distinguished golden retriever.

Goku is a golden retriever with impeccable manners and opinions to match. Each day he shares one short video: a small piece of wise, slightly posh advice, straight to camera.

The videos are made with AI from photos of the real Goku, and every post is labelled as AI-generated. Goku Studio is a small personal project that posts these videos to Goku's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
