About the app

Goku Studio is a small personal app run by Goku's owner. It makes short AI-generated videos of Goku, a golden retriever, and posts them to Goku's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

The videos

Every video is generated with AI from photos of the real Goku and is labelled as AI-generated on each platform. The videos are light entertainment: Goku's "wise advice" is not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.
